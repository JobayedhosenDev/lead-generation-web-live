1. Lead-Centric Strategy & User Journey

The sole metric of success for this website is High-Intent Perth Qualified Enquiries. Rather than building a passive digital brochure, every architectural choice is engineered to guide local home and property owners toward a direct call-to-action.

⚡ Friction-Free Conversion Triggers

🎯 High-Intent Local Positioning

Tailored copy addressing Perth soil conditions, Waterwise WA irrigation standards, UV-resistant synthetic turf, and suburb geotargeting.

📈 Enterprise GA4 Tracking

Silent dataLayer event tracking for every phone click, quote step completed, and form submission linked to Google Ads & Meta Pixel.

2. Tech Stack & Recommended Plugins

To balance lightning-fast mobile loading speed with an intuitive staff CMS, we propose a headless or hybrid WordPress architecture:

CMS & Framework

  • Core CMS: Custom WordPress 6.x (or High-Performance Static HTML/JS)
  • Page Builder: Elementor Pro / Custom Gutenberg Blocks
  • Theme: Hello Elementor (Lightweight 0.4s base load time)

Lead Capture & Forms

  • Form Engine: Gravity Forms / WPForms Pro
  • Calculator Engine: Custom JS Estimator + Webhook Sync
  • CRM Integration: Joblogic / Zapier / HubSpot Sync

SEO & Performance

  • Local SEO: RankMath Pro + Local Business Schema
  • Caching & Speed: WP Rocket + Cloudflare CDN
  • Analytics: Google Tag Manager & GA4 API
